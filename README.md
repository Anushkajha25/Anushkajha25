### Featured work

- **[Gait condition classification](https://github.com/Anushkajha25/gait-condition-classifier)**:
  classifies walking condition (normal, carrying a bag, wearing a coat) on CASIA-B with frozen
  ResNet101 + EfficientNetB2 features, PCA and an MLP head. A tested, reproducible rebuild of our
  B.Tech research. It replaces the original frame-level split, which leaked frames of the same
  walk into train and test, with a subject-disjoint evaluation.
- **[Water-quality classification](https://doi.org/10.1201/9781003773801-5)**: published chapter
